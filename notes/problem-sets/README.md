# Problem Sets (LearnChemE)

Joshua Sharpes's hand-worked solutions to chemical engineering screencast problems, mostly from **LearnChemE**, produced by the Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, Joshua's school (B.S. Chemical Engineering, 2023).

## Go to the source
For the problems themselves, worked video solutions, interactive simulations and self-study modules, use LearnChemE directly:

- Screencasts: https://learncheme.com/screencasts/
- Simulations: https://learncheme.com/simulations/
- Self-study modules: https://learncheme.com/quiz-yourself/interactive-self-study-modules/
- FE/PE exam review and student resources: https://learncheme.com/student-resources/

## The sets on the portfolio

- **LearnChemE: Thermodynamics** (EOS, phase diagrams, mixture thermodynamics, VLE, activity coefficients): https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app/slides/learnchem-thermo/
- **LearnChemE: Heat Transfer** (conduction, convection, heat exchanger design, radiation): https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app/slides/learnchem-ht/
- **Problems: Kinetics & Thermo** (rate laws, Arrhenius, equilibrium constants, Van't Hoff equation): https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app/slides/yt-kinetics/
- **Problems: Separations** (distillation, absorption, liquid-liquid extraction, membranes): https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app/slides/yt-separations/
- **Problems: Fluid Dynamics** (pipe flow, pumping systems, compressible flow, non-Newtonian): https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app/slides/yt-fluid/
