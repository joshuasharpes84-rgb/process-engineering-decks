### Slide 4: Sir George Stokes
- Physics and Mathematics Professor – Cambridge
- Numerous Theorems:
- Navier-Stokes Equation:
  - Derivation of Cauchy Momentum Equation with assumptions for incompressible, Newtonian fluid
- Stokes-Einstein Theorem:
  - Baseline assumption for diffusion of large spherical solute particles in solvents
  - Led to core diffusion heuristics and specific Wilke-Change correlation
- Stokes Flow:
  - For spherical particles with low Reynolds values, drag coefficients and terminal settling velocity correlations were established
- Also had numerous contributions to the field of calculus

### Slide 5: Alois Kremser
- Research Engineer – Standard Oil – Richmond, CA
- This engineer discovered the most fundamental method to estimate the number of theoretical trays for an absorption target.
- Work was posted in the 1930 Petroleum Journal: "Theoretical Analysis of Absorption Process"
- This method was refined by Souders and Brown in the1930s to yield the Kremser-Souders-Brown relationship.
- Photo Not Found
