### Slide 2: Venturi Meters
- Gradual contraction of cross-sectional area results in velocity increase at Contrera. Pressure taps on both sides of Contrera reports flow using systematic correction factors to account for frictional losses.
- Best used for low pressure applications such as gas lines or water mains or for fluids with significant suspension of solids.
- Location of pressure taps is dependent on phase and orientation (vertical/horizontal)

### Slide 3: The Dall Flowmeter
- Slotted throat annulus gives this design a lower permanent pressure drop for the same flow rates than the Venturi meter but a higher localized pressure drop at the contraction which makes it more susceptible to flashing/cavitation.
- Should not be used for settling liquids
- Gives similar accuracy to Venturis (+/- 0.5%)

### Slide 4: Orifice Plate
- Design is highly sensitive to perturbations and must be designed correctly:
  - Perfectly centered contraction
  - Upstream contraction edge should be sharp/squared (this device can not measure bi-directional flow)
  - Downstream pressure tap must be 1” from contraction to capture accurate pressure recovery
  - Meter flanges/gaskets will induce erroneous measurement
- Device is best suited for vapor and gas flows at entrance of pipes
  - Not advised for viscous or settling services

### Slide 5: Flow Nozzles
- This device is used for high velocity applications as their rounded approach accommodates very high Reynolds numbers.
  - Very common in plants where capacity is increased with same piping or for high temperature applications such as steam (larger piping is exponentially more costly)
  - Also common for wet gas applications as devices are less prone to erosion
- Orifice coefficient is between orifice plate and venturi meter (comp)
- Not suitable for viscous fluids

### Slide 7: Pitot Tube
- Measure difference of static vs dynamic head to determine  velocity of flowing fluid
- There are several designs:
  - Single/double tube
  - Manometer vs Impact pressure backstop
- Two concentric tubes design (Double Tube with impact pressure connection)
  - Inner tube measures dynamic head produced from flow
    - Uses impact pressure connection which gets multiple radial readings
  - Outer tube measures static head from ambient conditions

### Slide 8: Positive Displacement Flowmeters
- These types are best suited for batch operations or mixing/blending systems; high accuracy (+/- 1%)
- Reciprocating Piston Meters fill a small chamber with fluid and count the rotations of the piston
- Rotary Piston Meters fill a cylinder with flow and displace through rotation of piston throughout cylinder
- Rotary Vane Meters using rotating pockets like a mill
- Calibrated Flow Nozzles: displacement of bellows

### Slide 9: Mechanical Flowmeters
- This type of flow meter accomplish measurement by means of drag force measurement
- Rotameters are used with drag buoy which rises with flow, best suited for applications with low flow rates
  - Metal Tube Rotameters  are similar but with distinction of magnetized float measurement
- Turbine Meters measure a sinusoidal signal generated as the result of rotation
  - can be used to measure two-phase flow
  - Can handle corrosive and abrasive fluids

### Slide 10: Magnetic Flow Meter Systems
- Orthogonal metal strip reads voltage induced from flowing (conductive) fluid per Faraday’s Law:
  - Electrodes on both sides of strip transmit voltage
  - Fluid needs minimum conductivity to induce fluid – beyond this, only large changes in conductivity will impact readings
    - This type of meter is not impacted by physical properties: viscosity , temperature, pressure
- Applications of this type are numerous
- Should be position for up flow
- Frequent maintenance is necessary due to strip/electrode getting coated with solids

### Slide 11: Ultrasonic Flow Measurement – Doppler Flowmeters
- Best for measuring liquids with suspended solids.
- Utilizes the doppler effect; sound frequency (transmitted/received) is correlated to flow velocity
- Requirements:
  - Min solids concentration
  - Min velocity
  - Uniform piping material
  - Low temperature
