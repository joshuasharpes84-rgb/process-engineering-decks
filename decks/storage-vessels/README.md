### Slide 3: Pressure/Fixed-Roof Tank Roofs
- Some of the most common roofs:
  - Flat,
  - Recessed Flat
  - Conical
  - Domed Roof

### Slide 4: Tank Appliances
- Conservation or free vents are used for natural inhaling and exhaling of tanks
- Hatches are used for gaging and sampling
- Mixers are used for foam induction
- All of these components are prone to leak

### Slide 5: Floating Roof Tank Improvements
- Desired for low boiling liquids
- Roof in direct contact with liquid leads to solar boiling
- Pontoons were installed withing the tank roof for adequate buoyancy – rests on top of liquid – and increased insulation
  - Reduces outbreathing
  - Single and double deck options available
- Vapor traps collect formed vapors and utilize them as insulation

### Slide 6: Conservation Tanks
- Used to store variable amounts of gas volume at fixed pressure
- Often used for excess storage through means of a manifold
- Past maximum fill, gas is vented
- Also known as gas holders:

### Slide 7: Open-Top Tanks, Reservoirs, Pits, and Ponds
- Open Ponds have seen diminished used in developed countries due to environmental concerns:
  - Prone to tar buildup on bottom due long residing oils
  - Increase vapor emissions (VOCs)
  - Increasing ground water contamination
