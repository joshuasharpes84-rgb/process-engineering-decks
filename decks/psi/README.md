### Slide 3: 3 Types of Process Safety Information
- These three classifications correspond to materials, production units, and process users, respectively.
- Chemical Hazards Information (Materials)
  - What chemical hazards exist under normal and abnormal operation?
- Process Technology Information (Production Units)
  - How does the technology and its equipment that contain these chemicals operate to mitigate these hazards?
- Process Equipment Information (Process Users)
  - How is the equipment constructed to reliably execute the desired operation?

### Slide 4: Chemical Hazards Information
- Sources of material hazards information:
- Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS): reactivity, flammability, toxicity, explosive limits, heat of combustion (bomb calorimetry),
  - Primarily fixed data points
- Deflagration/Detonation Index, Max Pressure Rise
- Corrosive Data
- Calorimetry Data – Heat of Combustion (DSC, ARC)
- Minimum Ignition Energy (MIE)
- Thermodynamic Data: heat capacities, vapor pressures, thermal conductivity, densities
  - Primarily condition-dependent data
- Physical Data: viscosity, critical properties, henry’s law data, kinetics,
- Material Compatibility (reactivity)
- Miscellaneous Hazards: pyrophoricity, shock sensitivity,

### Slide 5: Process Technology Information
- These are sources that describe how a unit is intended to operate:
- Cause and Effect Matrix (SIS Lifecycle Management System – IEC Standards)
- PHA findings and IPL accreditation (PHA Management System – usually integrated into EMDS)
  - Consequences from Deviations
- Process Packages from Design Stage (Engineering Document Management System EDMS) – see next slide; there are two types
  - Control Philosophy
  - Design Conditions
  - Material and Energy Balances
  - Process Flow Diagrams
  - Maximum Intended Inventory
- Alarm Logic (Alarm Management System within DCS)
- Process Chemistry (Internal R&D System)
  - Adiabatic Reaction Temperatures
- Technology Patents (United States Patent and Trademark Office – USPTO)
- Pressure Relief Strategy (Relief Management Database)

### Slide 6: Process Design Knowledge Management
- There is a difference between Design Basis and Engineering Design Package:
  - Design Basis: Entire FEL project work
    - Equipment Specifications
    - Design Standards
    - Drawings
    - Hazard Analysis
    - Vendor Information
    - Quality Control
  - Engineer Design Package: FEL project work executed by EPC
    - Equipment Specifications (actual)
    - Periodic Integrity Testing
    - Initial Functional Testing
    - Mechanical Integrity Program Expectations

### Slide 7: Process Equipment Information (mostly in EDMS)
- These show how process users are constructed:
- Equipment and Instrument Files, Equipment Design Standards
- Foundation/Support Files: Structural Analysis
- Electrical Diagrams
- Piping Specification Documentation
- Piping and Erection Diagrams (Isometric Drawings)
- Plant Layout: Safety Showers, Electrical Classification,
- P&IDs, PFDs, BFDs
- PSV, PVSV Data Sheets
- Safety Interlocks
