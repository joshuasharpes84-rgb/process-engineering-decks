# Process Engineering Decks

Narrated PowerPoint decks by **Joshua Sharpes, EIT**, covering equipment sizing and selection, heuristics, and process safety. Companion material to the [technical portfolio](https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app).

**How to use:** download the `.pptx` and open it in **PowerPoint** (desktop). The decks have embedded audio and video walkthroughs that play in Slide Show mode, so you can work through each sizing workflow step by step alongside the narration. Browser viewers (including GitHub's preview) can't play the audio and won't open files this large.

All files are on the [**decks-v1 release**](https://github.com/joshuasharpes84-rgb/process-engineering-decks/releases/tag/decks-v1). Large decks (200–500 MB) take a minute to download.
